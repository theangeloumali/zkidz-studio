# What a fault looks like

Check the preview stills at every beat against this list. Fix the worst three, preview again, and stop only when a full
pass finds nothing.

- **A plate behind a word.** Any coloured box under a big word or a number.
- **Mostly-empty paper.** A heading at the top and cream below for more than a second.
- **Type on his face.** Anything between his hairline and chin; check the frames where he leans in.
- **Text half-hidden or on text.** A word behind his head or hair, or two labels, a kicker and a chip, or a caption and a
  heading sharing space at the same moment.
- **The room instead of the speaker.** A letterboxed shot of the whole room, or the camera resting on someone who is
  listening.
- **A face left unblurred.** When the user asked for other people to be blurred, any face but the creator's that reads.
- **A label that repeats the next heading.** A chip saying what the paper heading says a moment later.
- **A dead stretch.** More than about 1.5 s of footage with nothing new while he names something.
- **A pin off its word.** A count landing before the number is said, a strike before "no".
- **A device repeated.** The same technique twice back to back, or one carrying the edit.
- **A static camera.** More than about 6 s of footage with no zoom.
- **A thing named but shown as text.** He names money, an age, a tool or a market and the screen shows only the word.
- **Brand slips.** Gold used as text colour, white on gold, steel blue anywhere, the logo missing on the open card.
- **A reel out of bounds.** Under 120 s or over 180 s with its branding, or padded to reach the minimum.
- **A drifting frame.** A reframe window that moves mid-shot instead of on a cut or a zoom.
- **The intro seam.** The brand intro lands mid-word instead of on a pause.
